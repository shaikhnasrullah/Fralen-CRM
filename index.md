# FRALEN CRM - Optical Shop Management Software

FRALEN CRM is optical shop management software to manage customers, prescriptions, orders, inventory, purchases, billing and sales reports in one place. It is built for optical retailers and works in the browser and as an Android app.

Website: https://www.fralencrm.in/

## Manage your optical business smarter

Fralen CRM is a Firebase Firestore-based web shop management system built for optical retailers. Keep your products, customers, and orders organized, all in one place.

- [Get started or log in](https://www.fralencrm.in/Login-page.html)

## Key features

From inventory to customer management, Fralen CRM gives you the tools to save time and grow your business.

- **Product Management:** add, update and organize your optical products with ease.
- **Customer Management:** keep track of your customers, preferences and purchase history.
- **Order Tracking:** manage orders from checkout to delivery, all in one place.
- **Inventory Control:** monitor stock levels and avoid out-of-stock situations.
- **Reports & Insights:** get a clear view of your business performance with simple reports.
- **Secure & Reliable:** built on Firebase Firestore with your existing credentials.

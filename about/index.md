# About FRALEN CRM

## What FRALEN CRM is

FRALEN CRM is web-based shop management software built for optical retailers. It brings products, customers, prescriptions, orders, inventory, purchases, billing and sales reports into one dashboard, so an optical or eyewear shop can run its day-to-day work without paper registers or scattered spreadsheets.

## What you can do with it

- **Product management:** add, update and organize frames, lenses and other optical products.
- **Customer management:** keep customer details, preferences and purchase history together.
- **Order tracking:** manage orders from checkout to delivery in one place.
- **Inventory control:** monitor stock levels and avoid running out of popular items.
- **Reports and insights:** see how the business is performing with simple sales reports.

## Plans and pricing

New shops can start with a 15 day trial for Rs 1, after which the CRM plan is Rs 499 per month. Paid plans are CRM at Rs 499 per month or Rs 4999 per year, and CRM + Telle Calling at Rs 699 per month or Rs 7999 per year, which adds telecalling and customer follow-up. See the [pricing section](https://www.fralencrm.in/#pricing) for the current plans.

## How it is built

FRALEN CRM runs in the browser and is also available as an Android app. Sign-in uses Firebase Authentication and business data is stored in Cloud Firestore, with each shop's data kept under its own account.
